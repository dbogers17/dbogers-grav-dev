# Space Explorer Art Direction

The shared visual language is top-down industrial science fiction with sharp readable silhouettes, layered metal, small functional modules, practical docking ports, solar arrays, navigation lights, wear, shadows and faction-specific wealth levels. Helix Cooperative uses clean silver alloy, blue ceramic panels, amber lighting and organized circular station layouts. Future factions must vary structure, maintenance, colour, materials and module density while preserving gameplay readability.
